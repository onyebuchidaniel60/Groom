# Groom — User Guide (Planned Product Behaviour)

## For grooming businesses

### Create a study
1. Create an account with your business email.
2. Open your dashboard and select **Create experiment**.
3. Enter a clear title and short participant instructions.
4. Select one study type: hairstyle OR beard styles.
5. Choose three or four available curated styles.
6. Select a response target from 10 to 50 (default 20).
7. Save as draft and review the options.

### Publish and invite participants
1. Publish the study.
2. Copy its share link.
3. Invite adult customers or a relevant audience through your existing channels.
4. Pause the study if you need to stop new entries; close when you are finished.

### Understand results
- Results unlock after five completed responses.
- Review average rating, rating distribution and first-choice share for each style.
- Hair-texture segments (if enabled) are shown only when a segment has at least ten responses.
- Results represent the people who participated. They are not a statistically representative market survey or a guarantee of commercial success.

### Participant privacy
You will not see individual participants' photos, rendered images, identities or individual votes. Groom shares aggregate results only.

## For participants

1. Open the study link.
2. Read the instructions and privacy notice.
3. Confirm you are at least 18 and actively consent to photo processing.
4. Upload one clear front-facing JPG/JPEG photo showing your face and hair/beard area.
5. Wait while the previews are generated.
6. Compare all options. AI previews are visual simulations and may differ from a real barber result.
7. Rate each style from 1 to 5 and select one favourite.
8. Submit once and see the confirmation page.

You do not need an account, name, email or phone number. Your photo is temporarily processed for the preview and is not shared with the business owner. Groom's precise retention and Perfect Corp processing disclosures will be shown in the published Privacy Notice before participant data collection begins.

## Photo guidance

- Use a recent, clear photo of yourself.
- Only one adult face should be visible.
- Face the camera; avoid extreme head angles.
- Use good, even lighting.
- Avoid hats, sunglasses, heavy filters or anything obscuring the face.
- For hairstyle studies, keep the full hair and shoulders visible.
- For beard studies, keep the whole face visible; beard API has a stricter minimum face size and pose requirement.
- Use JPG/JPEG under 10 MB.
